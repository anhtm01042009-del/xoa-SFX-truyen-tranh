XOA CHU TRUYEN TRANH (ban Mac, chip Apple M1 tro len) - DOC TRUOC KHI DUNG

1. Keo XoaChuTruyenTranh.app vao thu muc Ung dung (Applications).
2. Bam doi de mo. Lan dau Mac se chan vi app chua co chu ky cua Apple:
   - Bam Xong / OK o thong bao.
   - Mo Cai dat he thong -> Quyen rieng tu & Bao mat, keo xuong duoi,
     bam "Van mo" (Open Anyway) canh dong noi ve XoaChuTruyenTranh,
     nhap mat khau may, roi bam Mo. Chi lam mot lan.
   - Neu Mac bao app "bi hong" (damaged): mo Terminal, go dong sau roi Enter:
       xattr -cr /Applications/XoaChuTruyenTranh.app
     sau do mo lai app.
3. Lan dau mo, app hoi tai mo hinh AI (khoang 290 MB, can mang, chi tai mot lan).
4. Bam "Mo anh..." hoac keo tha anh vao cua so, roi bam "Xoa chu".
   Ket qua tu luu vao thu muc Hinh anh (Pictures)/XoaChuTruyenTranh, ten co duoi _clean.png.
   App khong bao gio ghi de anh goc.
5. Cho nao con sot chu: bat "Khoanh vung", khoanh quanh cho do, app tu xoa.

Chi dung cho truyen tu ve hoac truyen ban co quyen chinh sua.

Gap loi: chup man hinh va gui kem file
  ~/Library/Application Support/XoaChuTruyenTranh/nhat-ky-loi.txt
(trong Finder: menu Di -> Di toi thu muc..., dan duong dan tren)
