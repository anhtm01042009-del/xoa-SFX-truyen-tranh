XOA CHU TRUYEN TRANH - DOC TRUOC KHI DUNG

1. Bam doi XoaChuTruyenTranh.exe trong thu muc nay de mo app.
   (Giu nguyen ca thu muc, dung chi chep rieng file exe ra cho khac.)
2. Lan dau mo, app hoi tai mo hinh AI (khoang 290 MB, can mang, chi tai mot lan).
3. Bam "Mo anh..." hoac keo tha anh vao cua so, roi bam "Xoa chu".
   Ket qua tu luu vao thu muc Pictures\XoaChuTruyenTranh (ten file co duoi _clean.png).
   App khong bao gio ghi de anh goc.
4. Cho nao con sot chu: bat "Khoanh vung", khoanh quanh cho do, app tu xoa.

Neu Windows hien "Windows protected your PC": bam More info -> Run anyway.

Chi dung cho truyen tu ve hoac truyen ban co quyen chinh sua.

Gap loi: chup man hinh va gui kem file
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\XoaChuTruyenTranh\nhat-ky-loi.txt
cho nguoi lam app.
